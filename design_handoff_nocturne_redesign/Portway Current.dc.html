Portway as it stands today

Every value below is lifted from the repo, not from memory: colours from values-night/brand_colors.xml, type from styles.xml, geometry from the layout XML, and the surface material from widget/ClayCardView.kt.

One thing worth seeing drawn out: ClayCardView pads itself by blur + offset = 21dp on every side, so a match_parent card inside 24dp screen padding actually paints its face 45dp from the screen edge, and two tiles in a weighted row sit 42dp apart. That gutter is reproduced here at true size — it is the single biggest reason the screens read as loose.

Known gaps in this recreation, in the interest of honesty: the ToggleSwitch is Material's stock switch, drawn here from the M3 dark tokens rather than from a Portway asset; the detail screen's config label/value rows inherit framework defaults in the app, so I have rendered them at the intent (muted label, onSurface value); and setShadowLayer blur is approximated with a CSS box-shadow.
